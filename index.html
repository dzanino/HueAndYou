One photo, three steps

  1. 1

    Take a photo

    A selfie or a picture from your library, in daylight. A guide helps you frame your face and warns about blur or backlight.

  2. 2

    The app measures

    Skin, hair, eyes and the contrast between them are measured in CIELAB, right on your device.

  3. 3

    You see why

    Your color type, hair shades, makeup and wardrobe, each with the measurements behind it. Then try them on your photo.

Everything your colors can tell you

Concrete guidance from your own measurements, not a quiz.

Your color type

Spring, summer, autumn or winter, anchored in your measured undertone, depth and contrast.

Try it on your photo

Hair color, lipstick, blush, eyeshadow, a T-shirt or a shirt, right on your picture. Combine them and hold the photo to compare.

Hair shades in your natural zone

Candidates within two levels of your natural depth, each with the why, a match score and a note for your colorist.

Makeup for your type

Foundation base by undertone, plus blush, lipstick and eyeshadow in the colors of your type.

Wardrobe and jewelry

Colors that lift you, your neutrals, colors to handle with care, and the metals that suit you.

Measured and explained

Measurements use CIELAB, with ITA°, hue angle and ΔL* contrast. The app always shows the numbers behind the verdict.

iPhone and iPad

Made for both, including Split View and Slide Over on iPad.

Shortcuts and Siri

Take a selfie, measure your colors or open your results with a shortcut, and by voice where Siri supports your language.

10 languages

English, Slovak, German, Spanish, Ukrainian, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Polish and Czech.

AI summary on the device

Optional. On devices with Apple Intelligence, an on-device model turns your measurements into a short personal note.
